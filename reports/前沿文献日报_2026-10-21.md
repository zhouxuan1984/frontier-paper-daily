   - 📄 摘要：We develop new methodology to improve our understanding of the causal effects of multivariate air pollution exposures on public health accounting for mobility. Utilizing flexible Bayesian methodology we develop novel estimation strategies to estimate causal effects that account for this spatial spillover.
