   - 📄 摘要：Cardiovascular diseases remain a leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide and impose a substantial clinical and socioeconomic burden. Despite advances in medical, interventional, and surgical treatments, considerable residual cardiovascular risk persists, highlighting the need for effective and sustainable therapeutic approaches.
