📄 Abstract Introduction: Brain shift reduces the accuracy of neuronavigation based on preoperative magnetic resonance imaging (MRI). Methodology: We propose NeuralShift , a U-Net-based framework that predicts a dense brain displacement field from preoperative MRI and resection laterality for patients undergoing temporal lobe resection.
