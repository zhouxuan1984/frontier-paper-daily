📄 Abstract Objective: To identify robust biomarkers for early cognitive decline, classification performance was evaluated for healthy controls (HC) versus mild cognitive impairment (MCI) using MEG-derived spectral features at the sensor and source levels, and by testing whether adding MRI anatomical features improves discrimination in the BioFIND cohort. Approach: Data from 324 participants (166 HC and 158 MCI) were analyzed using automated artifact rejection and source reconstruction. 📄 Robotic-assisted bronchoscopy (RAB) uses fluoroscopy and cone-beam CT (CBCT), which can expose procedural staff to ionizing radiation. Real-time, role-specific occupational dosimetry data from live RAB procedures remain limited. We prospectively collected continuous personal dosimetry from attending physicians, anesthesia providers, and scrub nurses during 51 consecutive RAB procedures at a single academic center using a standardized low-dose imaging protocol and shielding. Collar-badge readings were converted to estimated effective dose using a conservative upper-bound conversion factor. Role-specific dose distributions were compared, and scenario-based annual estimates were generated by bootstrap resampling. Patient BMI was independently associated with physician exposure. Projected annual exposures remained well below the ICRP 20 mSv occupational limit across all roles. Occupational radiation exposure during RAB was low across all staff roles under an optimized imaging workflow. Role-specific dosimetry captures exposure differences across the procedural team and supports standardized low-dose imaging and shielding as core safety practices.
